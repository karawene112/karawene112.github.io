Privacy Policy

Layover Logbook · Effective 12 November 2026

Layover Logbook ("the website") helps airline crew plan layovers: free time and rest, nearby places, spending and allowance, fatigue, and personal notes. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: no ads, no tracking, and your logbook is private to you. An account is optional.

1. Location

With your permission, the app reads your location only when you tap to use it, and only while the page is open. It never tracks you in the background.

To find nearby places, the app sends an approximate position (rounded to about 100 metres) and the category you picked to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de, with overpass.kumi.systems as a backup). Those services return a list of places. We do not receive, store or keep a history of your location. The operators of those services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

To show the name of your city, or to find a city or hotel you search for, the site uses the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), again with the approximate position only.

You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings (iPhone: Settings → Safari → Location). Searching by city and the logbook still work without it.

2. Your logbook and your account

Without an account, the layovers, spending, wallet, notes, wishlist and journal entries you save are stored only in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded and we cannot see them. Clearing website data, or removing the home-screen app, deletes them. Use the backup file in Notebook to keep a copy.

With an account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password, and a copy of your logbook so it follows you to a new phone. This is kept with our hosting provider Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Database rules allow only you to read or change your logbook. Hotel names and locations are never saved to your account.

You can delete your account, and everything saved in it, at any time from Notebook → Account and data → Delete account.

3. Crew feed

Posts, photos, ratings, likes and comments you share in the Crew feed are visible to every signed-in user, under the crew name you choose. Your email address is never shown. Choose a nickname if you do not want colleagues to recognise you.

Photos are resized on your phone before upload, which also removes the hidden location and camera data that phones add to photos. Photos are stored with Supabase in the European Union and can be viewed only by signed-in users. Do not post crew hotels, uniforms, other people's faces without their consent, or flight details.

You can delete any of your posts and comments at any time. Deleting your account deletes your crew name, posts, photos, comments and likes. Posts that other users report may be hidden and reviewed.

4. Payments

Layover Logbook Pro is sold through Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd), which acts as the merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment details and billing country directly. We never see or store your card number. Paddle tells us only your subscription status, plan, renewal date, and its customer and subscription reference numbers, which we store with your account. Paddle's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy policy at paddle.com.

5. Other services the site uses

  • Weather and local time: Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates of the destination city.
  • Exchange rates: ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). No personal data is sent.
  • Maps: map images come from CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com), based on OpenStreetMap data.
  • Directions: tapping "Directions" opens Google Maps.

These services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
