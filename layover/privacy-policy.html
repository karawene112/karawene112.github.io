Privacy Policy

Layover Logbook · Effective 18 November 2026 · Version française

Layover Logbook ("the website") helps airline crew plan layovers: free time and rest, nearby places, spending and allowance, fatigue, and personal notes. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: no ads, no tracking, and your logbook is private to you. An account is optional.

1. Location

With your permission, the app reads your location only when you tap to use it, and only while the page is open. It never tracks you in the background.

To find nearby places, the app sends an approximate position (rounded to about 100 metres) and the category you picked to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a public OpenStreetMap search service run by komoot, with two public Overpass servers (overpass.kumi.systems and overpass.private.coffee) as a backup. Those services return a list of places. We do not receive, store or keep a history of your location. The operators of those services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

To find a hotel or address you type, or a city, the site uses Photon (photon.komoot.io) and, as a backup, OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). It sends what you typed and the layover city, to show the closest matches.

You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings (iPhone: Settings → Safari → Location). Searching by city and the logbook still work without it.

2. Your logbook and your account

Without an account, the layovers, spending, wallet, notes, wishlist and journal entries you save are stored only in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded and we cannot see them. Clearing website data, or removing the home-screen app, deletes them. Use the backup file in Notebook to keep a copy.

With an account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password, and a copy of your logbook so it follows you to a new phone. This is kept with our hosting provider Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Database rules allow only you to read or change your logbook. Hotel names and locations are never saved to your account.

You can delete your account, and everything saved in it, at any time from Notebook → Account and data → Delete account.

3. Crew feed

Posts, photos, videos, ratings, likes and comments you share in the Crew feed are visible only to members of the feed, under the crew name you choose. Your email address is never shown. Choose a nickname if you do not want colleagues to recognise you.

Photos are resized on your phone before upload, which also removes the hidden location and camera data that phones add to photos. Videos are not re-encoded: the app wipes the location and description tags that phones write into video files before upload, but the recording date stays in the file. Photos and videos are stored with Supabase in the European Union and can be opened only by members of the feed. Do not post crew hotels, uniforms, other people's faces without their consent, or flight details.

The Crew feed is invite-only. To join you enter a one-time invite code from an existing member. We store which code you used and who gave it to you, so that an account that should not be there can be traced and removed. We do not ask for your airline, staff number or ID.

You can delete any of your posts and comments at any time. Deleting your account deletes your crew name, posts, photos, videos, comments and likes. Posts that other users report may be hidden and reviewed.

4. Roster check

Roster check (a Pro feature) compares a roster you add with flight time limits and estimates fatigue. The duties you type in or that are read from your roster, the limits you set and the results are stored only in your browser on this device. They are not saved to your account.

If you tap Read my roster, the screenshots you pick are made smaller on your phone, then sent over an encrypted connection to our server function at Supabase (European Union). From there they go to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI model Claude, which reads the duties and sends them back. We do not store the pictures, and they are only used to read your roster. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal), which say that data sent this way is not used to train its models by default. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time, for example to detect misuse.

Hide your name, staff number and anything else you don't want to share before you upload a roster. Roster check is a planning aid only. Your airline's approved scheme decides what is legal.

5. Payments

Layover Logbook Pro is sold through Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd), which acts as the merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment details and billing country directly. We never see or store your card number. Paddle tells us only your subscription status, plan, renewal date, and its customer and subscription reference numbers, which we store with your account. Paddle's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy policy at paddle.com.

6. Other services the site uses

  • Weather and local time: Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates of the destination city.
  • Exchange rates: ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). No personal data is sent.
  • Maps: map images come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), and the map viewer (Leaflet) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
  • Directions: tapping "Directions" opens Google Maps.
  • Destination photos: the postcard photo for each destination comes from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (en.wikipedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org). The app sends the name of the destination or landmark, nothing about you. Photos are saved on your phone after the first view.
  • 3D globe and fonts: the 3D globe uses the three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, and the lettering comes from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). No personal data is sent.

These services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

Visit counting. To know how many people use the site, each page view sends our own database the page address, the website you came from (its domain only, such as linkedin.com), your browser language, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and whether it is the first page of your visit. No cookie is used, and no IP address, account, identifier or precise location is stored, so a visit can't be linked to you. These counts are only used for statistics. If your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", nothing is counted.

7. Android app

The Layover Logbook app on Google Play opens this website full screen through Chrome on your phone. It works the same way as the website and collects nothing extra. Google Play may collect install and crash information under Google's own privacy policy. Pro can't be bought inside the Android app. If you already have Pro, sign in with the same account.

8. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • Spam protection works without tracking you: limits on how fast you can post, and checks on the sign-up form.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no cookies for measuring visits.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
