Land, open the app, know what to do.

Everything crew work out in their heads after a long sector, done for you in one place.

Your free time, worked out

Enter arrival and pickup. The app takes away transfer and rest, and shows the hours that are really yours.

  • Local time, home time and body clock side by side
  • Ideas for the city that fit how awake you are
  • Nearby food, transport and weather from where you stay

See fatigue before it sees you

Every trip adds up. Time zones crossed, nights landed in, short turnarounds: the app scores them and shows your week at a glance.

  • A 7-day fatigue load with a clear warning when it's heavy
  • Routes checked against public flight time rules
  • A guide only: your company's scheme and your own judgement come first

Know what you kept from every allowance

Log spending in any currency. It's converted to your home currency at live rates, so you see what each layover really cost.

  • Spending by category and by city
  • Allowance in, money out, and what you kept
  • Export everything to Excel

Where crew really go. Shared only with crew.

The crew feed is a private photo and video feed for layover cities: the café by the hotel that's open at 5am, the viewpoint worth the walk, the taxi trick that saves you money. Like it, comment on it, and post your own.

It's invite-only. You need to be invited by a crew member who already uses the app, and each invite code can be used only once. Every member can be traced back to who invited them. No passengers, no public profiles, no hotel names.
