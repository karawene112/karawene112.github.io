Questions

How do I pay?

Create a free account in the app, then choose Pro. Payment is handled securely by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Card details go to Paddle and never reach us. Prices are in US dollars; any sales tax or VAT for your country is shown at checkout.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, from the link in your Paddle receipt email or by emailing us. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of your first payment, or of a yearly renewal, and you get a full refund. Email us from the address on your account, or use the link in your Paddle receipt.

Who can join the crew feed?

The crew feed is for airline crew only. You join with a one-time invite code from a colleague who already uses the app. Pro does not replace the invite: it shows you every post once you are in.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your logbook, money entries, notes and posts stay in your account, and the free features keep working.

Which airlines is it for?

Any. You choose your own home base and currency. Layover Logbook is an independent project and is not affiliated with any airline.
