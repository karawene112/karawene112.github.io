Privacy Policy

Layover Logbook · Effective 29 October 2026

Layover Logbook ("the website") helps airline crew plan layovers: free time and rest, nearby places, spending and allowance, fatigue, and personal notes. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your logbook never leaves your phone.

1. Location

With your permission, the app reads your location only when you tap to use it, and only while the page is open. It never tracks you in the background.

To find nearby places, the app sends an approximate position (rounded to about 100 metres) and the category you picked to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de, with overpass.kumi.systems as a backup). Those services return a list of places. We do not receive, store or keep a history of your location. The operators of those services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

To show the name of your city, or to find a city or hotel you search for, the site uses the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), again with the approximate position only.

You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings (iPhone: Settings → Safari → Location). Searching by city and the logbook still work without it.

2. Your logbook

Layovers, spending, wallet, notes, wishlist and journal entries you save are stored only in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded to any server and we cannot see them. Clearing website data, or removing the home-screen app, deletes them. Use "Back up logbook" to keep a copy.

3. Other services the site uses

  • Weather and local time: Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates of the destination city.
  • Exchange rates: ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). No personal data is sent.
  • Maps: map images come from CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com), based on OpenStreetMap data.
  • Directions: tapping "Directions" opens Google Maps.

These services may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, under their own policies.

4. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
